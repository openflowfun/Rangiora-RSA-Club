Our Restaurant

Run by the club, loved by the community

The Spitfire Restaurant is run in-house by the Rangiora RSA Club. It's open every day and attracts diners and groups celebrating their special occasions - you don't need to be a member to enjoy it.

Whether it's a quick lunch, a family dinner, or a table for a milestone birthday, the Spitfire team will look after you. Takeaway available too.
