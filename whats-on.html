Entertainment Sat 10 Oct

Oktoberfest

Ein Prosit to good times! 1.30pm – 4.30pm. Live music from our very own Rangiora RSA Brass Oompah Band. 3-course lunch, stein and a drink included, German beers to purchase, fun and games. $60 a ticket – purchase tickets from the bar.

Live Sport 10 & 17 Oct

Bledisloe Cup: All Blacks v Australia

Live on the big screens. Saturday 10 October, 7.10pm, and Saturday 17 October, 6.00pm.

Live Sport 17, 24 & 31 Oct

Black Ferns v France

Live on the big screens. Saturday 17 October, 2.05pm; Saturday 24 October, 2.05pm; Saturday 31 October, 7.10pm.

Entertainment Sun 18 Oct

Showstoppers – The Showbiz Singers

Showbiz Christchurch performs at the Rangiora RSA Club. 11.00am. General admission $10 – scan the QR code on the poster to book.

Community Sun 25 Oct

Car Boot & Market Day

Rangiora RSA Club Carpark, 82 Victoria Street, Rangiora. 9am – 1pm. Proudly supported by Compass FM.

Entertainment Tue 10 Nov

NZ Cup Day

From 12pm. All races on the big screen, sport prizes and sweep stakes – join us, dress up and enjoy the day. Buffet lunch with a glass of bubbles, $35. Tickets available at the bar.
