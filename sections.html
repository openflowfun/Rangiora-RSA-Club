Get Involved

Club Sections

Ten sections, one community - there's a club night for everyone. Pick one (or a few) and get amongst it.

Find Your Club Night

Something for everyone

All sections are open to Rangiora RSA Club members. Some charge a small annual subscription or match-night fee to cover competition and equipment costs - details are noted below. Get in touch with a section contact, or ask at the bar, to come along.

D

Darts

Pop along on a Tuesday night throughout summer and winter, or a weekend if you fancy a social game - we're never far from the dart boards. The Darts Section enters teams in the Summer & Winter Darts Competitions in the North Canterbury area. Equipment advice is available for anyone keen to get started.

IB

Indoor Bowls

Played at the club since 1957. The season runs March to October, with play on Monday evenings from 7pm - no fee to join the section, just a $2/person mat fee each time you play. The section competes in North Canterbury, Champion of Champions and inter-club competitions, and is always keen for new members.

President: Valda Hopkins · 03 327 9976
Secretary: Trish Milne · 03 313 0158
Treasurer: Merv Willis · 03 327 9976
Club Captain: Wayne Milne · 03 313 0158

S

Snooker

Snooker and pool tables are available in the Sports Bar for members and guests any time the club is open. For section details, get in touch with the contact below.

Section contact: Jordan Beswarick · jordbez@gmail.com

F

Fishing

Formed in November 2005 and open to all club members - men and women - for $20 a year. Members fish everything from fly fishing to surfcasting, with competitive and social trips throughout the year at spots from Kaikoura to Gore and Lake Brunner to Otematata. The section meets every Friday night at the club to share stories and sell raffles.

G

Golf Section

Over 50 members of all abilities, open to all club members for $20 a year (financial year 1 June – 31 May). Monthly outings are held on the 2nd Sunday for the Colleen Cherry Memorial Trophy, plus "Best of Year" awards presented at the June AGM, a subsidised away trip, and an RSA Welfare fundraising tournament each March. The club has hosted the NZ RSA Golf Tournament in 2011, 2015, 2017 and 2022.

President: Nigel Seaton · Secretary: Mike O'Connor · Treasurer: Gary Clark

P

Pool

The season runs April to August, with two teams entered in the Rangiora Kaiapoi Pool Association Competition on Wednesday evenings, and a Ladies team competing on Monday evenings against other North Canterbury clubs and hotels. The Sports Bar has two pool tables, a snooker table and a ¾-size snooker table available for members and guests any time.

W

Welfare

The Welfare Section is actively involved in assisting members in a wide variety of areas, organised through the RSA Association which runs alongside the club.

Sec/Treasurer: Bill Peck · 03 359 3807

WS

Women's Section

Formed in 1934 to support the benevolent work of the RSA - a role it continues today through fundraising and voluntary work for members and their families. Open to all lady members of the club; come along and meet others while sharing in the club's facilities and outings in the community.

President: Pat Youngman · 027 247 9611

L

Lifestyle

The Lifestyle Section - get in touch with the section contact below for the latest on meetups and activities.

Section contact: Charlotte · rgarsafunctions@gmail.com

V

Veterans Section

Open to any veteran or supporter, operating under the same arrangements as the club's other sections. The section's major event each year falls around Vietnam Veterans Day (18 August).

Contact: Chris Gunn · clangunn@snap.net.nz · 027 845 4937
Committee: Pat Duggan QSM, Ian "Blue" Caldwell, Bill Hansen, Chris Gunn
