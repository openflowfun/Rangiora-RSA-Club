Served in the Forces?

If you have taken the Oath of Allegiance and served in the Armed Forces at any time - overseas service, home service during WWII, compulsory military training, National Service, Volunteer Territorials, Regular Force or Police - you are eligible as a service member. Record your service and service number on the application form.
