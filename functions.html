Booking Basics

Good to know

Bookings & Deposits

Tentative bookings are held for 7 days. A non-refundable deposit confirms your booking, and final numbers are due 3 working days before your event.

Catering & Beverages

All beverage service is provided by the club - no external alcohol permitted. Weddings need 14 days' notice for specific beverage selections.

Membership & Sign-In

Functions need enough members to sign the group in (one member may sign in up to 10 guests) - otherwise a special licence is required.

Courtesy Van

Available Thursday, Friday & Saturday from 4.30pm, run by our volunteer drivers. Call 03 313 7123 ext. 1 to book your pick-up.

Smart, neat presentation is required at all times. Full terms and conditions - including dress code, decoration policy and security - are available from the Function Team on request.
