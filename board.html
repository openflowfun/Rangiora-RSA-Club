How the Club is Run

Your board, your club

The Rangiora RSA Club Inc. is governed by a board elected by the membership. The board is responsible for the strategic direction and good management of the club, approves membership applications, and acts under the club's registered Constitution and Rules. Board updates are shared in the club and in the news.

The Rules We Play By

Constitution & Rules

The club operates under its registered Constitution and Rules. Every membership application is made subject to these rules and is accepted or declined by the Board. A copy is available at the club office.
