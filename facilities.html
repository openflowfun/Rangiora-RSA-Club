Our Club

Everything under one roof - lounge bar, sunny courtyard, gaming and sport, right in the heart of Rangiora.

The Heart of the Club

Club Main Bar

Our fantastic main bar offers a range of seating from 2 to 10 people, tables and bar leaners. With a large projector screen for watching sport, or space for larger groups to meet. Relax and enjoy with a large range of food and drink options with award winning service.

  • ✿ Full bar service in a relaxed, friendly atmosphere
  • ✿ Daily fresh baked scones and great coffee from 10am
  • ✿ Bar meals daily from 11.30am

Indoor & Outdoor

Poppy Room & Courtyard

A great indoor/ outdoor area - plenty of space in the sun, or sit under the louvres while watching your favourite sport on the outdoor screen while the children enjoy the playground facilities.

  • ✿ Sunny outdoor courtyard with louvre cover
  • ✿ Outdoor big screen for live sport
  • ✿ Playground for the children to enjoy

Getting Home Safely

Courtesy Van

The club operates a courtesy van for members - ask our friendly bar staff or phone the club for availability and pick-up times.

Call 03 313 7123

Safe Rides. Stronger Community.

Book your pick-up

Call us to book your pick-up on 03 313 7123 ext. 1. The van runs Thursday, Friday and Saturday from 4.30pm.

  • Gold coin pick-up service – donations are greatly appreciated to support the continued operation of the van.
  • Ticketed drop-off service – $3 per person within the 7400 Rangiora area, $5 per person for out-of-area rides. Tickets available at the bar.
