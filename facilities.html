The Heart of the Club

Main Lounge Bar & Courtyard

Our fantastic main lounge bar opens into a great outdoor courtyard - plenty of space in the sun, or sit under the louvres while watching your favourite sport on the outdoor screen.

  • ✿ Full bar service in a relaxed, friendly atmosphere
  • ✿ Sunny outdoor courtyard with louvre cover
  • ✿ Outdoor big screen for live sport
  • ✿ Bar meals daily from 11.30am

Functions & Venue Hire

Three private rooms, endless occasions

The Rangiora RSA has a number of rooms suitable for meetings, reunions, conferences and special occasions. Talk to us about special deals, group bookings and planning assistance for your function or meeting.

  • ✿ Boardroom - seats 12, perfect for meetings
  • ✿ Private function rooms - for mid-size gatherings
  • ✿ Main function room - seats up to 200 guests
  • ✿ In-house catering by the Spitfire Restaurant
