Welcome

A friendly place,
for any occasion

The Rangiora RSA Club is a short 30-minute drive north of central Christchurch in semi-rural Rangiora - within walking distance of the main shopping precinct, close to accommodation and everything the Waimakariri District has to offer.

  • Fantastic main lounge bar opening into a sunny outdoor courtyard - relax under the louvres and watch sport on the outdoor screen.

  • Gaming, pool, darts & more - 18 modern gaming machines, TAB, ATM, Sky TV and plenty of screens for live sport.

  • Three private rooms - from a 12-seat boardroom through to our 200-seat main function room.

Six rooms. Endless occasions.
